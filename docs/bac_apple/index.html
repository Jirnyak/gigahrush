Reynolds Flocking & Spatial Attractors

Particle steering combines classical boids separation $\vec{F}_{\text{sep}}$, alignment $\vec{F}_{\text{ali}}$, and target spring field $\vec{F}_{\text{target}}$:

$\vec{a}_i = w_s \sum_{j \ne i} \frac{\vec{x}_i - \vec{x}_j}{\|\vec{x}_i - \vec{x}_j\|^2} + w_a (\bar{\vec{v}} - \vec{v}_i) + k_t (\vec{T}_i - \vec{x}_i) - c \vec{v}_i$

Vector Silhouette Sampling

Complex silhouette contours (Apple, Touhou witch hat, DNA helix, Skull) are discretized into 2,000 analytical Poisson-distributed coordinate targets $\vec{T}_i \in \mathbb{R}^2$.

$\Phi(t) = (1 - \lambda)\vec{T}_A + \lambda \vec{T}_B, \quad \lambda \in [0, 1]$

Spatial Fidelity Metric

Real-time alignment fidelity $C(t)$ quantifies the mean normalized Euclidean distance between active boids and their designated silhouette anchors:

$C(t) = 100 \times \left(1 - \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \frac{\min(\|\vec{x}_i - \vec{T}_i\|, R_{\text{max}})}{R_{\text{max}}}\right)$
