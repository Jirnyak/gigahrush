# GIGAH|RUSH

⭐ **[RATE THE GAME ON ITCH.IO / ОЦЕНИТЬ ИГРУ](https://tenevik.itch.io/gigahrush/rate)** ⭐

**Browser survival horror / ARPG shooter inside an endless concrete apartment block.**

**GIGAH|RUSH** is a free HTML5/WebGL browser game about survival and expeditions through a self-rearranging Soviet-style concrete megastructure. You start in a safer living area, prepare food, water, ammo, medicine, documents, weapons or PSI tools, then follow quests, rumors, contracts or faction leads into dangerous, unpredictable floors.

Every expedition asks the same practical question: *What can you carry in, what can you bring back, and what happens if the floor changes before you return?*

The active floor is a living simulation surface. NPCs trade, sleep, fight, hide from Samosbor and can die permanently. Factions react to your actions, zones can change control, prices shift dynamically, caravans move resources, and consequences persist through browser saves as dead bodies, empty containers, battle marks, rumors, and reputation changes.

The central disaster is **Samosbor**. It strikes with alarms and sirens: hermetic doors seal shut, deadly fog leaks through cracks, psychological whispers echo, monsters awaken, shelters become life-or-death, and sections of the floor physically mutate after the storm passes.

## Features

- **Play directly in your browser:** Zero install, instant start, pure HTML5/WebGL.
- **Expedition Survival Loop:** Prepare supplies, ride the vertical lift routes, make hard choices, survive Samosbor, and return with loot or live with the consequences.
- **1024x1024 Toroidal Megastructure:** Massive seamless floors with flats, corridors, elevators, industrial sectors, void zones, and persistent floor memory.
- **Authored & Procedural Floors:** Story floors, faction headquarters, black markets, and procedurally assembled anomalous sectors.
- **A-Life NPC Simulation:** Persistent NPC identities with faction allegiances, daily routines, barter inventories, personal relationships, and permanent death.
- **Dynamic Economy & Factions:** Barter trading with supply/demand price multipliers, theft, faction reputation, caravans, and territory control.
- **Samosbor Disaster Events:** Siren warnings, sealed blast doors, fog leaks, monster incursions, psychological whispers, and cellular floor reconstruction.
- **Deep RPG Systems:** Vital stats (Hunger, Thirst, Sleep, Restroom, PSI, HP), inventory grid, equipment, weapon handling, leveling, and quest journal.
- **Procedural Audio & Visuals:** Procedural textures, procedural sprites, procedural atmospheric sound/music, WebGL raycasting, and canvas HUD.
- **Full Mobile & Desktop Support:** Intuitive dual-stick touch controls for mobile/tablets and keyboard/mouse precision on desktop.

## Controls

- **Click game area** — Capture mouse look
- **WASD** — Move
- **Mouse** — Look / Aim
- **E** — Interact, talk, open doors/containers, inspect
- **Left Mouse Button / Space** — Attack / Shoot
- **I** — Inventory & Character Stats
- **M** — Minimap / Full Map Overlay / Off
- **Q** — Quest Journal
- **L** — Message Log
- **F** — Faction Relations
- **Tab** — Control rebinding
- **U** — UI scaling & settings
- **Enter / Esc** — Game menu / Back / Close windows
- **F11** — Fullscreen

*On mobile/touchscreens, use the responsive on-screen movement/look sticks, attack button, [E] interaction popup, and top menu rail.*

## How to Start

1. Collect initial supplies (food, water, bandage, weapon) from the living area apartments.
2. Talk to local residents and administration to gather rumors, jobs, and permits.
3. Step into the central elevator lift and choose an expedition floor.
4. Watch your vitals and return to safety before Samosbor sirens seal the blast doors!
